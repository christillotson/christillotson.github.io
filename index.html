Remote Sensing

Agricultural Change in Loudoun County

For my Remote Sensing class, our final project was a mock scientific study. For my project, I chose to study agricultural change in Loudoun County by combining raster agricultural data with remotely sensed Landsat images of the county, and used a Tasseled Cap index of Wetness, Greenness, and Brightness change. I found an interesting North-South divide in the county and a general shift towards less labor intensive crops such as grain production, which historically was what Loudoun county had specialized in, as well as apparent increased urban sprawl expansion.

ArcGIS Pro & Python

GAGEWestUS_RTK

For my Advanced GIS class, I developed python script which works with ArcGIS Pro to automatically calculate which RTK stations are best for a theoretical planned UAV / UAs flight's RTK network connection. This was also an exercise in creating a GIS strategy plan, which was a plan uniquely tailored to the complexities of a GIS project including sourcing data, values and principles to guide the process, and value statements for stakeholders who would be affected by such a project.
